About Us

Made for clients who want custom prints done properly.

Intrinsic Designs specialises in custom made 3D printed models for creators, hobbyists, professionals, and businesses. From visually striking display pieces to practical prototypes and replacement components, each project is produced with attention to detail, material suitability, and print quality.

We work with key production details such as model dimensions, material type, infill density, layer height, quantity, and finishing requirements so that the final result matches both the intended use and the desired appearance.
